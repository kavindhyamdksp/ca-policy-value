Target: ≥12 interviews in the DECISION.md mix — ≥6 covered-facility analysts or finance leads, ≥4
consultants/ESCOs, ≥2 CCUS/CCfD developers. Aim to contact ~35 to land 12–15.

**No personal data is committed to this repository.** Keep the contact list (names, emails, employers)
in a private spreadsheet outside git. Here, record only aggregate counts.

## Screening (ask before booking)

1. Have you evaluated, approved or advised on an industrial decarbonization project in Canada in the last
   24 months? (must be yes)
2. Which province(s) and pricing system(s)?
3. Your role in that decision: analysis, approval, advice, financing?
